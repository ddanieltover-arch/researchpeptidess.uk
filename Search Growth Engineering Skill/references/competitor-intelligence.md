# Competitor Intelligence & Search Market Mapping

## Purpose
Use this layer to distinguish business competitors from search competitors, SERP competitors, paid competitors and AI-source competitors.

## Competitor classes
- **Business competitor**: competes for the same customer/problem.
- **Organic/SERP competitor**: repeatedly appears for target queries, even if the business model differs.
- **Paid competitor**: appears in supplied paid-search evidence for relevant queries.
- **AI-source competitor**: is cited as a source in observed AI-search results for relevant queries.

Do not collapse these classes into a single competitor list.

## Market map
Use repeated domain presence across supplied SERP observations to estimate search-market visibility. Weight stronger evidence with:
- frequency of appearance
- top-3 and top-10 presence
- query diversity
- AI citation presence
- paid-search presence

This is a competitive signal, not a market-share measurement unless the supplied data supports that inference.

## Keyword gaps
A keyword gap is strongest when:
- the target domain has no observed ranking for the query, and
- one or more competitors have a strong observed ranking, preferably within the top 20.

A ranking gap exists when both target and competitor rank but competitor position materially exceeds target position.

Do not create pages solely from gaps. Validate intent, business value, existing coverage and page purpose.

## AI-source competitor analysis
Track domains repeatedly cited by AI systems for relevant queries. Treat this as observed source competition, not a claim about permanent AI ranking.

Analyze:
- query overlap
- source-domain frequency
- target citation gaps
- recurring source patterns
- evidence/authority characteristics of frequently cited pages

## Paid/organic overlap
Use overlap to identify:
- queries where paid and organic strategies may compete
- terms where paid demand exists without organic visibility
- terms with strong organic performance where paid coverage may be unnecessary

Any spend or budget recommendation must use actual ad-performance evidence or be labeled as a hypothesis.

## Change monitoring
Compare market maps over time to detect:
- new competitor entrants
- competitor disappearance
- rising/falling SERP presence
- rising/falling AI-source citations
- increased paid presence

Change detection is a signal for investigation, not an automatic explanation.
