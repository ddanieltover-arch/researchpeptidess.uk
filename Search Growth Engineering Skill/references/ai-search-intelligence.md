# Advanced GEO/AIO & AI Search Intelligence

AI-search optimization is an evidence discipline, not a guarantee of visibility.

## Evidence levels
- OBSERVED: a measured result from a connected provider or recorded AI-answer observation.
- VERIFIED: supported by authoritative platform documentation or directly inspected project behavior.
- INFERRED: a reasoned interpretation of observations.
- HYPOTHESIS: a strategy that requires validation.

Never collapse these categories.

## Google generative search
Google Search Central's current documentation treats generative AI features as part of Search and continues to emphasize crawlability, indexability and useful content. Search Console also has generative-AI performance reporting for a subset of properties, including AI Overviews and AI Mode. Use those first-party reports when available rather than inventing AI visibility metrics.

## ChatGPT search
Public sites can appear in ChatGPT search. For pages intended to be discoverable/cited, verify that OAI-SearchBot is not blocked and that the host/CDN permits it where appropriate. A crawl permission check is not evidence of ranking or citation. Do not promise placement or citations.

## AI-answer observation methodology
For a stable query set:
1. Store query, engine, locale, timestamp, answer surface, source URLs/domains, citation status and evidence source.
2. Repeat queries over time rather than treating one observation as a permanent ranking.
3. Track target-domain presence, citation presence, competing source domains and source-type patterns.
4. Compare observations only when query, locale and answer-surface conditions are sufficiently comparable.

## Entity intelligence
Build an entity graph from first-party pages and authorized evidence. Track organization, people, products, services, locations and relationships. Never invent entities or relationships.

## Opportunity generation
Look for:
- repeated competitor citations for relevant queries where the target is absent
- missing first-party evidence for important claims
- unclear entity relationships
- weak answerable sections
- missing original data or expertise
- AI-search accessibility problems such as blocked relevant crawlers

Do not create pages solely to manipulate AI answers. Prefer original, useful, well-supported information.

## Monitoring
Track observed AI-search signals separately from Google Search Console metrics. Do not combine them into one metric unless the source and definition are explicitly documented.
