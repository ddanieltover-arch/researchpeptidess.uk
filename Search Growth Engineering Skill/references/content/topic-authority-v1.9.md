# Content & Topic Authority Intelligence v1.9

Use evidence to evaluate topical coverage, semantic/entity relationships, internal-link architecture, content decay and information gain.

Do not equate word count with quality. Longer content is not inherently better. Evaluate intent satisfaction, coverage, usefulness, original evidence, trust signals, entity clarity and internal relationships.

A topic authority score is an operational heuristic, not a search-engine metric. Use it to prioritize investigation, not to claim Google scores a site that way.

Content gaps must be validated against:
- real search intent
- existing page purpose
- business value
- competitor evidence
- technical/indexability constraints

Content decay is a signal for investigation. Check:
- seasonality
- algorithm/search-surface changes
- technical regressions
- SERP competition
- intent changes
- freshness requirements
- tracking changes

Information gain should favor original data, first-party experience, proprietary evidence, demonstrations, meaningful examples and useful synthesis. Do not manufacture uniqueness.

Internal-link recommendations should support user navigation and information architecture. Avoid manipulative link spam or irrelevant anchor text.
