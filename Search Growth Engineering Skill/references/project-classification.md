# Project Classification

## Classification dimensions

Return structured classifications for:
- asset type
- business model
- audience
- market
- geographic scope
- language scope
- acquisition motion
- conversion motion
- search maturity
- technical maturity

## Heuristic examples

### SaaS
Signals: recurring subscription, product/login, pricing, features, integrations, documentation.
Prioritize technical SEO, solution/use-case pages, comparisons, integrations, documentation, entity clarity, CRO and AI-search discoverability.

### Local business
Signals: physical locations, service area, calls/bookings/visits.
Prioritize local intent, location/service pages, business entity consistency, reviews/reputation, local structured data and conversion paths.

### Ecommerce
Signals: product catalog, categories, cart, checkout, inventory.
Prioritize category architecture, product pages, variant/indexation strategy, product structured data, images, feeds, commercial intent and performance.

### Marketplace/directory
Signals: user-generated listings, many filters, inventory or providers.
Prioritize indexation control, taxonomy, faceting, canonicalization, unique listing value and programmatic SEO only where user intent is genuinely distinct.

### Mobile app
Signals: iOS/Android package, Play/App Store metadata, deep links.
Prioritize ASO plus web discovery, app landing pages and brand/entity signals.
